Privacy Policy

Mano: PDF Scanner & Compressor · Effective date: October 30, 2026

The short version

  • Your documents stay on your phone. Scans, photos, PDFs and the text Mano reads from them are processed and stored on your device. We never upload them to our servers.
  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We use a few trusted services for crash reports, usage statistics, optional rewarded ads and subscriptions. They receive technical data, never your documents.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how AZM Developers ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Android app Mano: PDF Scanner & Compressor ("Mano" or "the app"). By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information that stays on your device

Mano is built to work offline. The following is created, processed and stored only on your phone. We do not collect it and cannot see it:

  • Camera images and scanned pages
  • PDFs and images you import, create, merge, split, sign, watermark or compress
  • Text recognised from your documents (used for automatic names, sorting and search)
  • Document names, folders, document kits and watermark templates
  • Your QR code scan history
  • Your signature images and app settings, such as theme

Camera: scanning uses the document scanner provided by Google Play services on your device. Pages are captured and processed on your phone and returned to Mano.

Private folder: if you lock it, Mano uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN unlock. Your biometric data is handled by Android and is never accessible to Mano or to us.

2. Information collected by third-party services

To keep the app working, fix problems and offer Premium and rewarded ads, Mano uses the services below. They may collect limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, device model, Android version, app version, an installation identifier Find and fix crashes
Google Analytics for Firebase App interactions (for example, which features are used), device type, approximate country, an app instance identifier Understand how the app is used and improve it
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device information, ad interactions Show rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and measure them
Google Play Billing and RevenueCat Purchase and subscription records, an anonymous app user ID, device and app information Process Premium purchases, restore them, and check your subscription status

We never receive your payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Privacy policies of these services: Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) and RevenueCat.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features and Premium subscriptions
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
  • To understand which features are useful and improve the app
  • To show rewarded ads when you choose to watch one
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Technical data is shared only with the service providers listed above, who process it to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

Files you share: when you share a document from Mano to another app (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Google Drive), that app's privacy policy applies to what you send.

Backups: if you create a backup, Mano saves one file to the location you choose, such as your Google Drive. It is stored under that service's terms. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

5. Data retention

  • On your device: kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall Mano. Uninstalling removes all of Mano's local data, except any backups or files you saved elsewhere.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics data: kept for up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed to provide and restore your subscription and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your documents at any time in the app, or uninstall Mano to remove all local data.
  • Ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Access or deletion requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal data held about you. Because Mano has no accounts, we hold very little. Contact us at the email below and we will help.

7. Children

Mano is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Your documents are stored in Mano's private storage on your device, protected by Android's security. Data sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but keeping your documents on your device means they are never exposed on our servers.

9. International users

Our service providers may process technical data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They apply their own safeguards when doing so.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
